# Zain Style Writing Pack — v1.0

## What this is

This pack captures the author's requested **Zain-style technical storytelling**: an engaging, connected, technically substantial article that helps readers *understand a system*, not simply learn its vocabulary. It grew out of earlier discussions of Zain Anwer-inspired explanatory videos and the author's repeated article reviews. It is an **original editorial specification**, not a reproduction of, or claim of endorsement by, that creator.

**Zain is not Zen.** Do not substitute the repository's [Zen Style Writing Pack](../Zen_Style_Writing_Pack/README.md). Zen favors a restrained explanation; Zain emphasizes reader curiosity, story momentum, causality, technically rich paragraphs, and visual teaching. Neither style permits hype or fabricated experiments.

## Default editorial promise

The reader should finish able to:

1. explain **why** the problem exists and why the obvious solution has limits;
2. trace **how** the relevant system works, including decisions, state, boundaries, and failures;
3. inspect a **concrete worked example**, source, or appropriately qualified experiment;
4. distinguish **measured, reported, and illustrative** claims;
5. decide **when to use the approach and when not to**.

The typical Medium feature target discussed previously was **8–10 minutes**, but this is a planning default, not a hard length cap. Deep investigations or complete build tutorials may take much longer. A 1,100-word synopsis is not automatically a finished article.

## Start a new article

Open [ARTICLE_WORKSHEET.md](ARTICLE_WORKSHEET.md), then [WRITING_PROMPT.md](WRITING_PROMPT.md). Approve the question, distinct thesis, evidence, technical path, and required figures before committing to an article. Draft section-by-section when instructed.

**If a practical implementation is promised, source files, execution evidence, environment requirements, and negative paths belong to the publication work—not to decorative companion material.** If it cannot be verified, narrow the article's claim honestly.
