# Zen Style Writing Pack

An editorial pack for teaching AI, software, data, and systems. Start with the guide, use the worksheet for the parts of the article that need planning, and keep `article.md` ready to hand to a reader.

| File | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| [Zen Style guide](ZEN_STYLE_GUIDE.md) | Set the writing, evidence, diagram, and discovery standards |
| [Article worksheet](ARTICLE_WORKSHEET.md) | Plan a draft and check it before publication |
| [Writing prompt](WRITING_PROMPT.md) | Brief a writing assistant on a new article or a revision |
| [Worked RAG article](examples/rag-policy-exception/article.md) | See a complete conceptual explainer with an original diagram |
| [Worked editorial notes](examples/rag-policy-exception/editor-notes.md) | See how the example’s claims, figure, and publication details were handled |

## Editorial references

The original kit informed the emphasis on explaining mechanisms, grounding claims in primary sources, and planning figures around reader questions. [Technical Article Editorial Kit](../Technical_Article_Editorial_Kit_v1_0/00_START_HERE.md)

The public openings of Toni Ramchandani’s articles supplied two teaching patterns. The Zed Delta piece gives the reader a concrete review decision. The FastAPI piece starts with a small endpoint that makes the scope of the tutorial tangible. Use that specificity whether the article explains a concept or teaches a build. [Zed Delta article](https://medium.com/data-and-beyond/zed-delta-vs-github-is-the-pull-request-era-coming-to-an-end-d505e5120b3f), [FastAPI article](https://medium.com/data-and-beyond/how-to-build-a-production-ready-generative-ai-service-with-fastapi-6b59f47fb744)
