# Prompt for a Zen Style Article

Supply the [Zen Style guide](ZEN_STYLE_GUIDE.md), the topic or current draft, and any relevant earlier articles or source material. Paste this prompt with those inputs.

```text
Write or revise a Zen Style article on [topic] for [audience, if known].
Use the supplied guide and relevant earlier articles. Identify the reader's
question and the distinct lesson this piece adds. Choose the article mode
that serves that lesson. Keep code, execution, and a companion repository
conditional on the evidence or reproducibility the article promises.

Open with the question or a concrete case. Explain the mechanism in reader
order. Give each substantive point one home. Tie factual claims to primary
sources, distinguish reported or observed findings from illustrations, and
keep material limitations next to the claims they qualify. Add at least one
finished diagram with a caption and alt text. Close with a concrete implication
or test that follows from the explanation.

Write one clear title, a subtitle that adds an angle, and specific section
headings. Use the terms readers search for where they fit naturally. Before
delivery, check the full piece for repeated points, factual drift, and broken
figure links.

Deliver clean article.md and its figure assets, or embed a complete Mermaid
diagram if working in a text-only handoff. Put any search metadata, evidence
gaps, and verification notes in a separate editor handoff. Resolve material
gaps by checking the source, narrowing the claim, or identifying the draft as
pending review in the handoff.
```
