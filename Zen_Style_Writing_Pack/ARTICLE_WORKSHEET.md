# Zen Style Article Worksheet

Fill in only the fields that inform the draft. Keep this worksheet with the editorial handoff.

## Frame the piece

- **Reader and starting knowledge**
- **Question they came to answer**
- **What they can explain, diagnose, or decide afterward**
- **Closest earlier article and the new contribution here**
- **Article mode** — explain, compare, synthesize, or build
- **Evidence and artifact choice** — what the reader needs to see, and whether a runnable example or companion repository is essential

## Check the complete article

- [ ] Read the title, subtitle, headings, captions, and ending in order. Merge repeated points and confirm the body delivers the title’s promise.
- [ ] Verify the evidence for the central claim, including any version, metric, quote, and observed result. Resolve or remove unsupported claims and keep material limits visible.
- [ ] Open the figures and links from the manuscript. Inspect the diagrams at phone width and check labels, captions, and alt text.
- [ ] Read `article.md` as a reader would see it. Remove editorial notes, draft scaffolding, and placeholders. If it promises a build, check that the reader has the versions, artifacts, and steps required to reproduce it.
