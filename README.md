# WritingGuide

An editorial system for original, technically rigorous articles about AI, software, and engineering. **The writing style and the technical quality gates are separate decisions.**

## Start here

| Document | Responsibility | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Zain Style Writing Pack](Zain_Style_Writing_Pack/README.md) | **Default narrative voice**: curiosity-led technical storytelling, connected prose, mechanism, examples, and earned conclusions | Medium articles, engineering essays, deep technical explainers |
| [Technical Article Editorial Master Playbook](TECHNICAL_ARTICLE_MASTER_PLAYBOOK_v1_0.md) | **Technical and evidence controls**: thesis, breadth/depth/height, source register, diagrams, verification, review gates | Every substantive engineering article |
| [Technical Article Editorial Kit](Technical_Article_Editorial_Kit_v1_0/00_START_HERE.md) | Separate templates, claim register, diagrams, prompts, and review protocol | Research and production workflow |
| [Zen Style Writing Pack](Zen_Style_Writing_Pack/README.md) | A distinct, more restrained explanatory approach | Explicitly requested Zen-style pieces or shorter conceptual explainers |

**Zain is not Zen.** These names refer to distinct writing approaches. The Zain pack records the author's repeatedly requested approach to Zain Anwer-inspired technical storytelling, not an assertion that the creator endorses or follows this guide. The Zen pack remains available, unchanged.

## Priority for Zain-style technical articles

1. **Factual contract:** The master playbook governs claim accuracy, primary sources, code verification, security, diagrams, and publication blockers.
2. **Narrative contract:** [ZAIN_STYLE_GUIDE.md](Zain_Style_Writing_Pack/ZAIN_STYLE_GUIDE.md) governs reader journey, story, pacing, depth, and prose.
3. **Article contract:** The approved brief controls the actual topic, reader, length, scope, practical promise, and section-by-section cadence.
4. **Publication contract:** SEO/AEO/GEO metadata must make the article findable without replacing clarity, evidence, or a truthful headline.

Where the two styles differ, do not silently switch from Zain to Zen. Do not reduce technical coverage merely to reduce word count. Use section-by-section drafting when requested; otherwise follow the approved brief.

## Default workflow

**Frame the question -> Research the evidence -> Storyboard the system and visual(s) -> Draft the complete argument -> Review adversarially -> Verify and publish.**

The practical path is in [the Zain article worksheet](Zain_Style_Writing_Pack/ARTICLE_WORKSHEET.md), [reusable prompts](Zain_Style_Writing_Pack/WRITING_PROMPT.md), and [editorial release gates](Zain_Style_Writing_Pack/EDITORIAL_REVIEW.md). Cross-check material claims using the [master review protocol](Technical_Article_Editorial_Kit_v1_0/06_EDITORIAL_REVIEW.md).

## Quick start

> Use the Zain Style Writing Pack as the narrative standard and the Technical Article Master Playbook as the factual standard. Research **[TOPIC]** using authoritative sources; produce a one-page thesis/reader brief, a causal section spine, a running worked example, an evidence map, and a figure storyboard. Challenge anything unsupported. If I request section-by-section drafting, stop after the requested section. Otherwise draft only once the brief is complete. Keep publication SEO/AEO/GEO metadata outside the article body.

**Not an endorsement or a reproduction of any third-party writer's prose.** The goal is an independent authorial voice, shaped by the author's requirements and verified engineering evidence.
